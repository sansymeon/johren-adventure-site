Where in the World
Is Turbo?

An adventure could be waiting just around the corner!

Turbo Adventures is a way to discover places across Japan, then collect puzzles and characters as you go.

Find a destination, check in when you are there, complete collectible pictures, and make scenes with the characters. Basic play is free and does not need a player account. Coverage is growing across Japan.

🗺️

Explore the Map

Use the map to find railway stations and see where Turbo and friends can be found.

🚉

GPS Check-in at Stations

One successful collection per station per device, until you clear saved collection data on that device.

🧩

Complete the Mosaic

Stations feed a railway mosaic. Levels are 5, then 10, 25, then 50 pieces. Finishing a level unlocks the next and the count starts again. Airports are a separate collection and do not add railway pieces.

🏪

Turbo Spots (Coming Soon)

Merchant QR discoveries are planned and marked Coming Soon. Participation will be free. Railway mosaic rewards stay separate from future merchant QR rewards.

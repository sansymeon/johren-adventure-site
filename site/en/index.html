Is Turbo?

An adventure could be waiting just around the corner!

Explore your world. Share your discoveries!

Turbo Adventures encourages you to explore the world around you, discover new places, rediscover familiar ones and share your discoveries with others. Collecting is part of the fun, but the real adventure is what you discover along the way.

Find a destination, check in when you are there, complete collectible pictures, and make scenes with the characters. Basic play is free and does not need a player account. Coverage is growing across Japan.

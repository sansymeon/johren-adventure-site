Privacy policy

24 November 2026. ターボアドベンチャーズ（Turbo Adventures）. Operated by サンシメオン・プロダクション（個人事業）.

When location permission is requested

The browser asks for location permission only when you use check-in on the map. Opening a page does not ask. If you refuse, check-in cannot run. The map can still be shown.

How GPS is handled

Latitude and longitude are used on that device to measure distance to a published pin. The check-in program does not send those coordinates to a Turbo Adventures server. A successful record stores the place id, the time, the distance in metres, and the accuracy in metres. It does not store the latitude and longitude.

The record is in the browser localStorage. The main key is turbo.locationCollection.v1. An older test key, turbo.gpsCheckin.v1, may still be present. Railway puzzle progress is turbo.railwayMosaic.v1. A completion-screen flag is turbo.finalReveal.v1.

Scenes

The public Create screen stores a scene draft in localStorage and can save a JPEG on the device. That screen does not send a name, email address, or scene to a Turbo Adventures server.

Cookies, analytics, and hosting

The pages do not include an analytics tag, and this service does not set a login cookie. The site is served by Netlify. Netlify may process connection logs (IP address, browser type, requested URL, and time) as the host. Opening the map loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap tile servers. Those servers receive the IP address and tile requests that show which part of the map is on screen.

Shop enquiries

There is no form on the site. Enquiries are email to info@sansymeon.com. The website program does not store that mail. Receiving and keeping it happens in the ordinary inbox for that address.

Deletion

Collections and scenes are on the device. Clearing the browser’s site data removes them. The operator does not hold a copy, so the operator cannot delete data on a device. To ask about email you sent, write to info@sansymeon.com.

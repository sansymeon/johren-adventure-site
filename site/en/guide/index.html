TURBO ADVENTURES · HOW TO PLAY

Check in at stations.
Complete the mosaic.

Japanese is the default. English is a language switch of the same experience. Players are Turbo Milers (ターボ・マイラー). Free, with no registration.

This page separates what you can do now from what is not offered yet.

A little note 🐢
We love the adventurous spirit of frequent travellers! But sorry, no miles or points here. Just memories, discoveries, and Turbo collectibles!

A Little Request from Turbo 🐢
Please be considerate of the people and communities around you as you explore! Respect local rules at stations, shops, shrines, temples, and other locations. Stay safe, and have fun discovering Turbo!

How to play

🗺️
01

Find a place on the map

Available. Railway stations, airports, scenic places, and cultural sites share one map. Coverage is growing.

📍
02

Check in on site

Available. GPS check-in works once per place in this browser. QR check-in for collecting places is not available yet.

🧩
03

Collect puzzle pieces

Available for railway stations. A station check-in adds a railway puzzle piece. Airports and scenic places are separate collections and do not add those pieces.

🖼️
04

Complete the picture

Available. Railway stations, airports, cultural and historic places, and scenic places are separate puzzles. The order is 5, 10, 25, then 50 pieces again and again. Finished pictures stay. Each new 50 starts at zero. A place already collected does not fill the next picture.

🎨
05

Collect characters and make a scene

Partly available. The cast page and arranging pictures on your device are available. Submitting a scene is not available yet, and neither is a QR reward for collecting places. A postcard QR is the shop enquiry.

Rewards stay separate

Discovery-puzzle pictures stay separate from shop listings. A postcard QR is an enquiry. The listing appears on the map after we review it.

How the puzzle grows

A new place in a category opens one piece of that category’s current puzzle. Railway stations, airports, cultural and historic places, and scenic places do not fill one another. The grids are 5 (one row), 10 (5 by 2), 25 (5 by 5), and 50 (10 by 5). After the first 50, every later picture is another 50. The finished picture stays, and the next 50 starts at zero.

The sample picture is Emperor Maximilian and Empress Anastasia at Frostbourne. Every level uses that same picture. Dark tiles are pieces not yet earned by a visit.

Three rules

Once per stationOn one device, a station counts once until you clear saved collection data. It is not a daily rescan.
Free, no registrationTurbo Milers play for free. No registration and no purchase.
Airports are separateAirport collections do not add pieces to the railway mosaic.

Questions

Do I scan a QR code?

Collecting places uses GPS check-in. A postcard QR opens the shop enquiry page. QR check-in for collecting places is not available yet.

Can I check in at the same station again?

Clearing saved collection data on that device lets you collect again. Until then, one success per station.

Can I save pictures?

Create lets you arrange a scene on the device and save a JPEG. The public screen does not send the scene to a Turbo Adventures server.

Shops can apply for a free listing. A postcard QR opens the enquiry. The pin is added to the map after the application is reviewed.

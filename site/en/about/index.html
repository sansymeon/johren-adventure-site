What is Turbo Adventures

Discover Japan, and collect as you go.

Turbo Adventures is a way to discover places across Japan, then collect puzzles and characters along the way.

Growing across Japan. The map, GPS check-in, puzzles for railway stations, airports, cultural places, and scenic places, the cast page, and making a scene on your device are ready to play. A free shop listing is added to the map after we review the application. A postcard QR is for that enquiry. QR check-in for collecting places is not available yet.

What you can do

Look for a destination on the map. Check in when you are there, and the place is saved on that device. Railway stations add pieces toward a collectible picture. You can meet the characters and arrange collected pictures into a scene.

Basic play is free and does not need a player account. Japanese is the default. English is the same service in another language.

About the operator

ターボアドベンチャーズ（Turbo Adventures） is an original place-discovery and digital collection service planned, produced, and operated by サンシメオン・プロダクション（SanSymeon Productions）.

SanSymeon Productions makes digital illustration, educational material, characters, and websites that present the character of local places.

The service

About Turbo Adventures

Japanese is the default. Check in by GPS at railway stations and collect the mosaic.

Live: GPS check-in at railway stations and the railway mosaic. Merchant QR for Turbo Spots is Coming Soon.

What this is

Turbo Milers (ターボ・マイラー) explore the map, collect at stations, view the collection, and complete mosaics. It is free, with no registration. English is a language switch of the same experience.

The railway mosaic

One successful collection per station per device, until you clear saved collection data. Levels are 5, then 10, 25, then 50 pieces. Completing a level unlocks the next and the count starts again.

Airports are a separate collection. They do not add railway mosaic pieces.
